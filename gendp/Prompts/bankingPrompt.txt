I want to add banking to the pe SPM. Right now it has 4 banks, but eventually I want it to have 8. 
Each bank will be 4K ints (so half of a current bank). When two PEs try to access the same bank,
there will be a bank conflict. In this case one of the PEs will stall, meaning it will not move it's
PC or execute any instructions until it is granted access. I will provide more detail and break down
the plan.

# milestone 4
Increase bank count.
We have SPM_BANK_SIZE and possibly other references to bank size. For the most part these refer to
the BANK_GROUP_SIZE and should be renamed. This BANK_GROUP_SIZE will remain 8k, but we will now
also have SPM_BANK_SIZE which will be 4k. Essentially, the bank group refers to a pair of banks.
This pair is high order interleaved with the other pairs, so it is "private" to a pe in virtual
addressing mode. However, the pair itself is low order bit interleaved. So
PE0 has  banks 0/1,
PE1 gets banks 2/3,
PE2 gets banks 4/5, 
PE3 gets banks 6/7,
A sequence "abcdef..." would alternate a to bank 0, then b to bank 1, c to bank 0, d to bank 1 and
so on until BANK_GROUP_SIZE after which q to bank 2, r to bank 3, s to bank 2, t to bank 3 etc
until 2*BANK_GROUP_SIZE
+ Start by renaming SPM_BANK_SIZE and related variables, and adding BANK_GROUP_SIZE

+ change the SPM portbusy logic to appropriately route the address to proper port and see if there
  is a conflict
